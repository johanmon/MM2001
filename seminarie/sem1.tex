\section*{Uppgift 1.7}

Sätt $f(x) = x^2$. Rita kurvorna:

\begin{multicols}{2}
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*]
  \item $y = f(x)$
  \item $y = f(x-1)$
  \item $y = f(x+1)$
  \item $y = f(x) - 1 $
  \item $y = f(x) + 2 $
  \item $y = f(2x) $
  \item $y = 1/2 f(X) $
  \item $y = -f(x) $
  \item $y = f(-x) $    
  \end{enumerate}
\end{multicols}
